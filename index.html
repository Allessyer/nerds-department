01 Why we exist

Brilliant students are everywhere. Opportunity is not.

A university with fewer resources should not mean fewer chances to meet ambitious peers, try difficult work, or discover what you can become.

We are building the student department we wish every university had: open, practical, collaborative, and driven by curiosity rather than credentials.

01

Pick a real problem

Choose work close to what actual teams do—not a practice assignment that disappears into a folder.

02

Learn while building

You do not need to know everything. Bring energy, be reliable, ask questions, and improve as you go.

03

Leave with proof

Ship something useful, receive feedback, and gain experience you can honestly discuss in an interview.
